01 — HOW IT WORKS
01

List the terms.

Owners set a daily rate, a refundable deposit, and a maximum rental period. The listing is written to BOT Chain.

02

Book onchain.

Renters choose dates and approve one transaction. The rental fee and deposit move into the escrow contract.

03

Complete and settle.

When the rental is completed, the owner receives the fee and the renter receives the deposit back automatically.

02 — THE AGREEMENT

The contract holds the money.
Rentora never does.

Every booking records the resource, renter, start and end times, rental fee, deposit, and current status. Anyone can inspect the contract on the testnet explorer.
